Robot Manipulation Benchmarks

A catalog of benchmarks for robot manipulation: physical benchmarks, hosted platforms, competitions, proxy evaluators checked against real robots, and the simulation benchmarks that VLA papers report on. Each entry says what it evaluates and which evaluation problem it sets out to fix.

Figures come from each work's paper or project page and remain the property of their authors; the detail view links to the source of every image.

Descriptions summarize survey notes taken in October 2026. Many 2026 entries are preprints, so check venue and status before citing. Simulation entries and policy results were compiled from automated reading of papers; check numbers against the paper before quoting them. Entries without a usable figure show a hatched plate.
